About TheDirtCalc

TheDirtCalc is a small, free collection of calculators for one recurring homeowner headache: figuring out how much topsoil, mulch, gravel, sand or compost a project needs before ordering it. Too little means a second delivery fee or a mid-project store run; too much means a leftover pile in the driveway. The math is simple — area times depth — but the conversions between cubic feet, cubic yards, tons and bags, and the fact that every material weighs a different amount per yard, trip people up every day.

Each calculator here uses a material-specific density drawn from commonly published supplier and extension-service ranges, shows that density on the page, and lets you change it if your local supplier works to different numbers. Results include the exact calculated quantity and a recommended order with a 10 percent buffer for settling and spills, plus a copy-ready shopping list in plain text.

TheDirtCalc is an independent project. It is not affiliated with any landscape supplier, and it does not sell material — the numbers are estimates to plan with, and final quantities should always be confirmed with your supplier, especially for large or structural work. The site is a work in progress: more materials and project guides are planned.
