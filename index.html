How these calculators work

Every landscape material — soil, mulch, gravel, sand, compost — is measured the same way underneath: volume equals area multiplied by depth. You measure the length and width of the space, choose how deep the material should sit, and the result is a volume in cubic feet. Divide by 27 and you have cubic yards, the unit that bulk suppliers sell by. Multiply by the material's density and you have the weight in tons, which is how gravel and sand yards price their loads. Divide by the size printed on a bag and you have a bag count. Each calculator on this site runs that chain for one material, with the right density and the right typical depths built in.

The densities are where cheap calculators go wrong, because a cubic yard of mulch (about 675 lb) and a cubic yard of gravel (about 2,700 lb) are wildly different loads. TheDirtCalc uses a separate, published-average density for every material — 75 lb per cubic foot for screened topsoil, 25 for shredded mulch, 100 for crushed stone, 96 for pea gravel, 90 for river rock, and so on — and shows the density on every page so you can adjust it if your supplier quotes a different weight per yard. The gravel calculator goes further and switches density by stone type, because angular crushed stone packs tighter and weighs more per yard than rounded river rock.

Each calculator also builds in a 10 percent buffer on its recommended order. That is not padding for its own sake: loose material settles and compacts after watering and traffic, ground is never perfectly level, and some always spills between the pile and the bed. Ten percent is the difference between finishing the project and making a second trip. Finally, every result comes with a plain-text shopping list you can copy into a note or a message to your supplier — area, volume, tonnage and bag counts, in the units each trade actually uses. If you are deciding how to buy rather than how much, the bulk vs. bagged calculator compares delivered bulk prices against bag prices using your local numbers.
