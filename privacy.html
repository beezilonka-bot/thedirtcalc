Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026. This page describes, in plain language, what information TheDirtCalc does and does not collect.

No accounts, no personal data. TheDirtCalc has no sign-up, no login and no checkout. All calculations run entirely in your own browser — the dimensions and prices you type into a calculator are processed on your device and are not sent to, or stored on, any server operated by this site. We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number or location, and there is no contact form that stores submissions.

Basic technical logs. Like virtually every website, the hosting provider may record standard server log information (such as IP address, browser type and pages requested) for security and reliability purposes. These logs are not used to build profiles of individual visitors.

Advertising. TheDirtCalc may in the future display advertising, including ads served by Google AdSense. If advertising is enabled, third-party advertising partners — including Google — may use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. You can opt out of personalized advertising through Google's Ads Settings page, and you can block or delete cookies in your browser settings at any time. If and when advertising is introduced, this policy will be updated to describe it in more detail.

External links. This site may link to other websites for reference. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those sites. Questions about this policy can be sent to the site operator via the contact details on the About page.
